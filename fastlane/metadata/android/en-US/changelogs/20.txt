Civil War and She-Hulk can be drawn.

A Civil War game is a pair — a side, Resistance or Registration, and the hero
you face — and the eight combinations are now offered, along with the two
She-Hulk ones. MarvelCDB has not entered these scenarios, so the app can name
the game to play but cannot show you a setup for it.

Each pair draws three or four modular sets, decided by the draw.

Those sets also stopped turning up where they do not belong. All fifteen Civil
War sets are in the card database even though its scenarios are not, so Hell's
Kitchen was drawable for Rhino. They are legal in Civil War and She-Hulk games
only.
