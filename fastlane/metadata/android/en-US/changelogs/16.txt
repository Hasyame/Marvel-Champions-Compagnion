The random draw no longer offers difficulties you have not got. Standard I and
Expert I come in the Core Set, Standard II and Expert II with The Hood, and
Standard III with The Age of Apocalypse — so a Core-only collection was being
told to play a difficulty sitting in a shop.

The scenario picker now names the pack under each scenario. The Green Goblin
pack contains "Risky Business" and "Mutagen Formula", so looking for the Goblin
found nothing even though both were there.

French set names MarvelCDB leaves in English can now be corrected in the app
rather than waited on. "Escape the Museum" is "Fuir le Musée".
