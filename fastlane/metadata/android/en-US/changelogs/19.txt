Your collection now answers for everything the app offers.

Scenarios can be ticked off in My collection, the same way modular sets already
could. A box split with a friend or bought second hand is missing scenarios as
easily as sets, and unticking one means you have not got it — so it disappears
from the random draw and from setting a game up by hand.

The 'Pool aspect only appears if you own Deadpool. Its cards came in that pack,
so without him there is no such aspect to build a deck in.

Both play screens now say so plainly, rather than leaving you to work out why
something is not on the list.
