Plays sent to BoardGameGeek can now say where they were played.

Set it once under Settings, where you play. It travels with each game, so
changing it later does not move games you played somewhere else.

Typed rather than sensed: BoardGameGeek records a location as free text a
person wrote, so asking Android for your position would mean a location
permission on an app that has only ever wanted the network, to produce
something less useful than the word you would have typed.
