Four bugs found by playing two real games, at two and three players.

The random draw follows your collection again: it used to read what you owned
once and never look again, so changing your packs left it drawing from the old
answer. The difficulty picker respects the filter it always claimed to.

A row could quietly stop rerolling: opening a picker to look and pressing OK
locked it exactly as if you had chosen something. It locks only on a real
change now.

BoardGameGeek was recording every multiplayer game as solo, because a play
carries as many players as it has player rows and only one was ever sent.
Everyone at the table now gets a seat, and the start and finish times are in
the comment.
