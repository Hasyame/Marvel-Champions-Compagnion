Sixteen French set names the card database gets wrong are now corrected in the
app: Fuir le Musée, Infiltrer le Musée, Dieu des Mensonges, Loi Martiale,
Scientifique Suprême, Recrues Dangereuses, Puissants Avengers, Jeunes Avengers,
Nouveaux Avengers, Héros à Embaucher, Jumeaux Écarlates, Garde Royale, Duo
Mortel, Atlantes, Défenseurs and L'Initiative.

They are generated from docs/game_contents.json, which now records what is in
every box — 72 scenarios and 99 modular sets across twelve waves, with the pack
each difficulty came in.
